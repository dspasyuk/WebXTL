Spherical Absorption Correction

                    
                

                

                    

                        
                        

                            
                            
                        

                        
Every intensity is multiplied by 1 / T(muR, theta). Enter muR
                            directly, or fit it to the current model (minimises R1).

                    

                    

                        
                        

                            
                            
                            
                            
                        

                        
Three dimensions, or a single one for a sphere diameter. Uses the
                            composition mu to convert the size into muR.

                    

                    

                

                

                    
                    
                

            

        

    


    
    

        

            

                

                    
Cluster Molecules & Relabel

                    
                

                

                    

                        

                            
                            
                        

                        

                            
                            
                        

                        

                            
                            
                        

                    

                    
Atoms are grouped into molecules by bond distances (covalent radii ×
                        bond factor, with periodic boundary conditions). Molecules are ordered by position in the cell
                        and atoms are renumbered per element in that order: e.g. two ethanol molecules become C1 C2 O1
                        and C3 C4 O2.

Change Occupancy (sof)

                    
                

                

                    

                        
                        
                        
Sets the SOF field (5th numeric column) of atom lines. Values above 10
                            (e.g. 11.0) refine the occupancy via the corresponding FVAR free variable.

                    

                    

                

                

                    
                    
                

            

        

    


    
    

        

            

                

                    
PLATON TwinRotMat

                    
                

                

                    
Analyses the Fo/Fc data for unaccounted (non)merohedral
                        twinning. The search is built from a temporary LIST-4 refinement of the current model.

                        search more permissive (useful when no twin law is reported).
